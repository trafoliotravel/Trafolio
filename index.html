📝 John’s local tips
🌦March is gorgeous — mild days and the first cherry blossoms. But pack a warm jacket: evenings still drop close to freezing.
⏰Heading to teamLab, Disney or Senso-ji? Book online and arrive right at opening — queues triple by midday.
🙏Keep your voice down on trains and in elevators, never tip, and don’t eat while walking — it reads as disrespectful here.

Simple by design.

Create a trip

Add places as you go
Log restaurants, hotels, sights — with your own rating and a personal note. Import photos from your camera roll, matched to the right day.

Share one link
Your travel profile is live at trafolio.travel/yourname. Send it to anyone. No app download needed.

Generate a beautiful photo album of your trip. AI writes the stories. You just print.
